About Me

I am a Full-stack Developer and IT Technician focused on building practical digital products with a clean structure and a strong technical foundation. My work so far includes responsive websites, custom business systems, workflow automation, and database-driven applications built with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Sequelize ORM, and Strapi.

I like to structure projects in a professional way by separating the user interface, backend logic, data layer, and automation flow so the solution is easier to maintain and scale. I also pay attention to version control, reusable components, and clear project organization, which helps me deliver systems that are reliable, easy to understand, and aligned with real business needs.
